# Content calendar

A calendar is a set of posts that each do a different job toward one goal. It is not the same post reworded five times.

## Steps

1. **Goal and period.** Take them from the brief; fall back to the brand goals. State the one outcome the period should move.
2. **Pillars.** Pick 2-4 themes that connect the audience's problems to what the brand can credibly say. Every post belongs to one pillar.
3. **Mix.** Across the period, vary the job of the post: teach (how-to, framework), prove (result, customer story, data), opine (a point of view the audience can disagree with), and ask (announce, invite). Roughly one "ask" for every three or four of the others.
4. **Cadence.** Default to 3-5 posts per week per channel unless the brief says otherwise. Spread them over weekdays, and schedule in the audience's working hours. Never schedule in the past.
5. **Draft every slot.** For each slot load the `social-post` skill and write the actual post, then save it with `save_draft`. A calendar of titles is not a deliverable.
6. **Check the set.** Read the drafts as a sequence: no two with the same opening move, no repeated claim, and the first post makes sense with no context.

## In the rationale of each draft

Say which pillar and which job the post serves, so the person approving can judge the plan and not only the wording.
