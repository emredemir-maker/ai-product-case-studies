## Product Lens

The common thread across these case studies is not AI as automation alone.

The focus is AI as a product layer that helps teams:

- structure scattered signals,
- reduce operational friction,
- improve decision quality,
- make work more measurable,
- keep human judgment and accountability in the right place.
